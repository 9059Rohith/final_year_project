\begin{frame}{Retrospective: Panel 1 Proposal Overview}
    \vspace{-3mm}
    \begin{block}{Initial System Architecture (Panel 1)}
        \scriptsize
        For our initial defense, we proposed a highly ambitious, multimodal edge Al system for ASD speech therapy in rural South India. The core components included:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item \textbf{Multimodal Real-Time Processing:} Simultaneous processing of acoustic features (Wav2Vec 2.0) and visual stress markers (MediaPipe facial landmarks).
            \item \textbf{Advanced Scaffolding:} Immediate Day-1 deployment of a Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG) reinforcement learning agent for continuous difficulty adjustment.
            \item \textbf{Privacy Engine:} Utilizing Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) to secure federated weight aggregation across devices.
            \item \textbf{Longitudinal Tracking:} A Semantic Knowledge Graph (OWL/RDF triples) to map phonetic biomarker trajectories (VSA, GOP) over time.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}

    \vspace{2mm}
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{The Engineering Bottleneck}
        \scriptsize
        We hypothesized that this entire pipeline could execute on low-power edge devices with an end-to-end inference latency of \textbf{< 200ms}. However, critical panel feedback correctly identified that running FHE, a Transformer model, live video processing, and an RL agent simultaneously on edge hardware was a \textit{technological contradiction}.
    \end{block}
\end{frame}


% ============================================================
% SLIDE 2: FEEDBACK AND FIXES FROM PANEL 1
% ============================================================
\begin{frame}{Panel 1 Feedback \& Strategic Architectural Revisions}
    \vspace{-4mm}
    \begin{columns}[T, totalwidth=\textwidth]

        \begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
            \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
            \begin{block}{1. The Latency \& Hardware Contradiction}
                \tiny
                \textbf{Critique:} Claiming <200ms latency while running FHE, DDPG, MediaPipe, and Wav2Vec on the edge is hardware-impossible.\\
                \textbf{Resolution:} We aggressively stripped overhead through a phased deployment:
                \begin{itemize}
                    \item \textbf{FHE Dropped:} Replaced with standard Federated Learning (Flower framework) where raw audio is deleted and only mathematical gradients sync.
                    \item \textbf{Phased Rollout:} DDPG shifted to Phase 2 (activates after 22,500 episodes via a Rule-Based engine first), and MediaPipe pushed to Phase 3.
                \end{itemize}
            \end{block}

            \vspace{0.5mm}
            \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
            \begin{block}{2. Knowledge Graph Redundancy}
                \tiny
                \textbf{Critique:} An OWL/RDF Knowledge Graph is computationally heavy and redundant for simply tracking scores.\\
                \textbf{Resolution:} Eliminated the KG. Transitioned to a lightweight, scalable \textbf{MongoDB Aggregation Pipeline} using hierarchical session documents to detect recurring syllable weaknesses automatically.
            \end{block}
        \end{column}

        \begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
            \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
            \begin{block}{3. CNN vs. Transformer Placement}
                \tiny
                \textbf{Critique:} Why use two models (CNN \& Transformer), and where do they live (Edge vs. Cloud)?\\
                \textbf{Resolution:} Clarified that they are components of a \textit{single} backbone (Wav2Vec 2.0). CNN layers extract local retroflex bursts, while Transformer layers track long-range prosody. \textbf{The entire quantized model lives strictly on the Edge} to ensure zero cloud latency.
            \end{block}

            \vspace{0.5mm}
            \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
            \begin{block}{4. Architecture Diagram Clarity}
                \tiny
                \textbf{Critique:} The previous diagram lacked clear data boundaries and processing flow.\\
                \textbf{Resolution:} Completely redesigned the architecture mapping (Slide 8) to explicitly define the \textbf{Edge Device Boundary} (all audio processing, local SQLite) vs. the \textbf{Server Boundary} (anonymized payload syncs, federated weight aggregation).
            \end{block}
        \end{column}

    \end{columns}

\section{Introduction}
\begin{frame}{Problem Statement \& Need of the System}
    \vspace{-2mm}
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small Problem Statement}
        \small 
        To develop a privacy-first, edge-based AI speech therapy system for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) in rural South India. Existing cloud-based AI tools fail on Dravidian retroflex consonants and atypical ASD prosody, while risking pediatric privacy. The proposed offline system addresses the critical shortage of therapists by providing precise, syllable-level pronunciation feedback and tracking longitudinal progress securely on-device.
    \end{block}

    \vspace{2mm}

    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small Need of the System}
        \footnotesize
        \begin{itemize}
            \setlength{\itemsep}{4pt}
            \item \textbf{Addressing the Therapist Scarcity:} With fewer than 1 therapist per 10,000 children in rural Tamil Nadu, this system empowers a single remote therapist to effectively monitor 20-30 children.
            \item \textbf{Clinical Precision (Syllable-Level):} Replaces binary whole-word scoring by evaluating exact syllable boundaries, telling therapists exactly where the articulation is failing.
            \item \textbf{Privacy \& Data Sovereignty:} Pediatric biometric voice data is highly sensitive. Processing and deleting raw audio entirely on-device ensures no data ever leaves the child's home.
            \item \textbf{Objective Progress Tracking:} Replaces subjective clinical observations with repeatable, data-driven acoustic metrics (VSA and GOP).
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Project Objectives}
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{Core Technical \& Clinical Objectives}
        \small
        \begin{itemize}
            \setlength{\itemsep}{6pt}
            \item \textbf{Edge Computing Pipeline:} To develop an offline AI pipeline that processes atypical speech entirely on-device without any cloud dependency, ensuring zero latency and absolute privacy.
            \item \textbf{Transfer Learning for Dataset Scarcity:} To adapt Wav2Vec 2.0 through a two-stage transfer learning process—first for Tamil phonetics, then for disordered pediatric speech.
            \item \textbf{Acoustic Space Normalization:} To implement Vocal Tract Length Normalization (VTLN) to map child audio into adult acoustic space, ensuring children are not penalized for their physiology.
            \item \textbf{Syllable-Level GOP Scoring:} To implement Goodness of Pronunciation (GOP) scoring that identifies exactly which syllable a child mispronounces within a complex word.
            \item \textbf{Longitudinal Metric Tracking:} To track Vowel Space Area (VSA) and GOP trends per child, delivering bi-weekly structured reports to remote therapists.
            \item \textbf{Adaptive Scaffolding:} To build a staged adaptive difficulty engine, transitioning safely from transparent rule-based control to Federated Deep Reinforcement Learning (DDPG).
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}
\end{frame}

\section{Novelty}
\begin{frame}{Our Novelty \& Architectural Innovations (1/2)}
    \vspace{-2mm}
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small 1. Syllable-Level GOP Scoring for Regional Languages}
        \footnotesize
        Current AI scores speech at the word level. We use Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) to align child audio to templates at precise syllable boundaries. Computing log-likelihood ratios per syllable isolates the exact articulatory failure for therapists.
    \end{block}
    \vspace{2mm}
    
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small 2. Two-Stage Transfer Learning}
        \footnotesize
        Lacking a Tamil ASD corpus, we fine-tune Wav2Vec 2.0 in two stages: first using AI4Bharat Kathbath for Dravidian phonetics, then using the TORGO dysarthric corpus as a validated proxy for disordered speech patterns.
    \end{block}
    \vspace{2mm}

    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small 3. VTLN Child-to-Adult Acoustic Mapping}
        \footnotesize
        Children's shorter vocal tracts produce higher formants than adult references. We apply a VTLN warping factor to normalize child audio into adult acoustic space, ensuring the model assesses actual pronunciation effort, not biological anatomy.
    \end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Our Novelty \& Architectural Innovations (2/2)}
    \vspace{-2mm}
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small 4. Staged Adaptive Difficulty (Rule-Based to Federated DDPG)}
        \footnotesize
        Phase 1 deploys an auditable rule-based engine that manages difficulty while aggregating interaction data. Phase 2 safely transitions to a DDPG reinforcement learning agent only after collecting enough data for stable policy decisions.
    \end{block}
    \vspace{2mm}
    
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small 5. Privacy-First Federated Learning Without FHE Overhead}
        \footnotesize
        FHE is too computationally heavy for low-cost edge tablets. We achieve zero-trust privacy by deleting raw audio immediately after on-device processing, syncing only mathematical model weight gradients to the aggregation server.
    \end{block}
    \vspace{2mm}

    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small 6. Automated Bi-Weekly Therapist Dashboards}
        \footnotesize
        A MongoDB aggregation pipeline queries session documents to detect recurring phoneme weaknesses. This auto-generates structured reports, alerting remote therapists exactly when specific consonant or vowel classes require intervention.
    \end{block}
\end{frame}

\section{Methodology}
\begin{frame}{Technical Knowledge: Core Algorithms in Action}
    \vspace{-2mm}
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small Wav2Vec 2.0 \& Dynamic Time Warping (DTW)}
        \footnotesize
        \textbf{Function in Pipeline:} Wav2Vec 2.0 extracts local retroflex bursts (CNN) and long-range prosody (Transformer). DTW then mathematically aligns the child's variably paced speech against healthy templates, isolating precise syllable boundaries.
    \end{block}
    \vspace{1mm}
    
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small Goodness of Pronunciation (GOP)}
        \footnotesize
        \textbf{Function in Pipeline:} Instead of transcribing text, GOP computes a statistical confidence score (log-likelihood) for each syllable. Scores below an optimal threshold flag specific articulation failures, providing granular, actionable clinical feedback.
    \end{block}
    \vspace{1mm}
    
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{\small Vowel Space Area (VSA) \& DDPG Scaffolding}
        \footnotesize
        \textbf{Function in Pipeline:} VSA tracks corner vowel formants; an expanding area indicates improved jaw/tongue motor control. This VSA trend, combined with real-time GOP scores, forms the "state" our DDPG agent uses to dynamically adjust the difficulty of the next therapy prompt.
    \end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Module Description (1/2): Edge Pipeline \& Feedback}
    \vspace{-4mm}
    \begin{columns}[T, totalwidth=\textwidth]

        % COLUMN 1: Input & Extraction
        \begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
            \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
            \begin{block}{1. Input \& VTLN Normalization}
                \tiny
                \textbf{Function:} Captures offline pediatric speech and maps the child's vocal tract to adult acoustic space to prevent physiological penalization. \\
                \textbf{Data Flow:} 16kHz mono audio flows into the VTLN Normalizer, where a warping factor $\alpha$ transforms the raw waveform before extraction.
            \end{block}

            \vspace{1mm}
            \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
            \begin{block}{2. Wav2Vec 2.0 Feature Extraction}
                \tiny
                \textbf{Function:} A two-stage fine-tuned edge model extracts local phonetic features (CNN) and long-range prosody (Transformer). \\
                \textbf{Data Flow:} Normalized audio is converted into high-dimensional feature embeddings and Mel Filterbank/LPC outputs for formant tracking.
            \end{block}
        \end{column}

        % COLUMN 2: Scoring & Scaffolding
        \begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
            \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
            \begin{block}{3. DTW Alignment \& Scoring Engine}
                \tiny
                \textbf{Function:} Mathematically aligns atypical, variably paced speech to reference templates to isolate precise syllable boundaries.\\
                \textbf{Data Flow:} Features are aligned with the Reference Syllable Bank. The engine outputs per-syllable log-likelihoods (GOP: 0.0-1.0) and Vowel Space Area (VSA) triangle coordinates.
            \end{block}

            \vspace{1mm}
            \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=amritaMaroon, fg=white}
            \begin{block}{4. Adaptive Scaffolding Control}
                \tiny
                \textbf{Function:} Evaluates real-time pronunciation success to adjust the difficulty of the next prompt (transitioning from Rule-Based to DDPG). \\
                \textbf{Data Flow:} The GOP Threshold Checker receives syllable scores. If avg GOP > 0.80, harder words are queued; if < 0.50, easier words are selected.
            \end{block}
        \end{column}

    \end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Conclusion}
    \vspace{2mm}
    \setbeamercolor{block title}{bg=black, fg=white}
    \begin{block}{A Targeted Solution for a Critical Gap}
        \small
        This project does not attempt to be a generic speech-to-text wrapper. Every technical decision traces back to a specific clinical or geographic gap. The system is engineered to provide the precision of a trained therapist inside the 3-to-8 year early intervention window, operating in environments where internet and specialized clinics are unavailable.
    \end{block}
    
    \vspace{2mm}
    \textbf{Summary of Technical Justifications:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{VTLN} exists because children are acoustically distinct from adults.
        \item \textbf{Two-Stage Transfer Learning} exists to bypass the total absence of Tamil ASD corpora.
        \item \textbf{Syllable-Level DTW} replaces clinically unactionable whole-word grading.
        \item \textbf{Federated Edge AI} exists because pediatric biometric privacy is legally non-negotiable.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{2mm}
    \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=4pt, center, rounded=true]{palette primary}
        \footnotesize \textbf{The Final Impact:} A system where a child in rural Tamil Nadu practices daily with real-time feedback, while a single therapist in Chennai monitors their articulatory progress securely and objectively.
    \end{beamercolorbox}
\end{frame}
